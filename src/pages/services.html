Why CHOOSE Devi - Tranquil Villa

Villa DEVI is a sanctuary of refined luxury blended with timeless heritage, offering thoughtfully curated amenities to ensure a peaceful and comfortable stay. Each room and space within the villa has been designed to immerse guests in an atmosphere of understated elegance, allowing for total relaxation and solitude.

Dining Area

The intimate dining area provides a dedicated space for enjoying meals, whether it’s a quiet breakfast or a leisurely dinner.

Kitchen & Pantry

Adjacent to the main villa, guests will find a fully equipped pantry and kitchen area. Here, the house caretaker or butler can prepare meals to meet guests’ tastes and preferences.

Fitness

Experience a peaceful 1-hour guided walk through the lush village, surrounded by paddy fields, tropical flora, and local charm. Immerse yourself in the sights and sounds of rural life while connecting with nature and tradition. Perfect for those seeking a gentle escape and authentic cultural insight.

Private Garden with BBQ Facility

Relax in your own private garden, a serene space perfect for outdoor dining and leisure. The built-in BBQ facility makes it ideal for fun cookouts with family or friends. Surrounded by greenery, it’s a perfect spot to unwind and enjoy the fresh air.

Complimentary Tea & Coffee

A selection of teas and coffee is provided to enjoy at any time of day.

Wifi & Internet

Guests can stay connected, with reliable WiFi available throughout the property. No Television, No Air Conditioning: Villa DEVI embraces a philosophy of simplicity and detachment from modern distractions, encouraging guests to disconnect. However, all rooms and common areas are equipped with fans to ensure comfort during warmer days.

Airport Pick-up

Enjoy a hassle-free arrival with our convenient airport pickup service straight to the villa. A friendly driver will greet you at the terminal and ensure a smooth, comfortable journey. Start your stay stress-free with reliable transport right to your doorstep.

Laundry Services

The villa offers a convenient laundry facility equipped with a modern washing machine for guest use. Whether for a quick refresh or an extended stay, keeping your clothes clean is easy and accessible. Drying space is also available, making the process efficient and hassle-free.

Villa Service

Enjoy dedicated assistance at the villa from 8 AM to 5 PM to ensure a smooth and comfortable stay. Our friendly staff is available to help with daily needs, local tips, and any special requests. Whether it’s housekeeping or travel support, we’re here to make your day effortless.
